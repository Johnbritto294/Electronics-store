What we stand for

Three rules we
never break

Show the real thing

Real photos, real specs and real limitations. If a product has a weakness, we will tell you before you buy it — not after.

Stand behind the sale

Our warranty and repair teams are in-house. When something goes wrong, you deal with the same people who sold it to you.

Buy less, choose better

We would rather help you keep a device for five more years than sell you a replacement you do not need.

Our story

From a garage
to your home

2012
One bench, one belief

Stackly starts in a garage, fixing friends' laptops and refusing to sell anything we would not use ourselves.

2016
The first real store

We open on Swarnapuri Main Road in Salem with four staff, a repair bench and a very optimistic coffee machine.

2020
Service goes in-house

Delivery, setup, trade-in and repairs all move under one roof so customers stop being passed around.

2026
48,000 customers later

Same obsession, bigger team: more than 300 specialists across stores, support and the workshop.
